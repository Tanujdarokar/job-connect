Transparency & Data Rights

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 28, 2025. Learn how CareerConnect respects candidate privacy and handles local data.

1. Overview & Architecture

CareerConnect is built on a privacy-first, client-side web architecture. All user accounts, saved jobs, bookmarked internships, and application tracking records are stored exclusively in your Web Browser's LocalStorage and SessionStorage.

We do not operate remote databases that aggregate or sell your personal career data to third-party ad networks or brokers.

2. Information Stored Locally

  • Account Profile Data: Full Name, Email Address, Professional Headline, and Location. Passwords are encrypted using SHA-256 Web Crypto hashing prior to local storage.
  • Application History: Job/internship IDs, application date, and status indicators (Applied, Under Review, Shortlisted, Rejected).
  • Bookmarks & Preferences: Saved opportunity IDs and color theme choice (Light vs Dark mode).

3. Cookies & Local Storage Consent

CareerConnect uses essential client-side LocalStorage entries required to provide functional features (such as user authentication, saving jobs, and preserving user theme preferences). You can clear or reset all local data at any time via your browser settings or using the "Clear All My Local Data" button in your Dashboard.

4. Data Security

We enforce strict security practices on the frontend, including HTML input escaping to prevent Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), Web Crypto hashing for passwords, and strict Content Security Policy (CSP) headers restricting script execution to self and trusted CDNs.

5. Your Rights & Data Erasure

Because your information resides on your device, you maintain 100% control over your data. You may export, update, or permanently delete your account records instantly from your personal Dashboard.
